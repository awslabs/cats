CATS proves your controls work

  • Make the call an SCP or IAM policy should block, and check that it is denied.
  • Send HTTP, TCP and UDP traffic between accounts, across as many accounts and regions as you point it at.
  • Tag a test with the control it covers, such as SOC2-CC6.1, and the report records it.
  • Let your AI agent verify IAM, service and network behavior before it writes a test, through cats mcp or cats run.
